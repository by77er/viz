A list of commands or choices on a `raised` surface. `vz-menu` with `role="menuitem"` rows and `role="separator"` dividers. A row may be a `div` or a real `button`; the class resets a button's own border, fill and alignment.

- Each row is a verb phrase, with its shortcut as a `vz-kbd` in `ink-secondary` at the right. The active row is `hover`; a press is `pressed`, painted at once. A row that opens a dialog ends in "…" ("Revoke key…").
- **A row that holds a choice** (the current time range, a sort order, a setting that is on) is `role="menuitemradio"` or `menuitemcheckbox` with `aria-checked`. Every such row reserves a tick column at its left; the chosen row has the tick, in `ink`, and is `ui-strong`. Not blue, not inverted. Do not mix checkable and plain rows without a separator between them.
- **A menu that confirms a cautionary choice** (Silence for how long): the choice rows, a separator, `vz-menu-note` (one or two `small` sentences in `ink-secondary` that say what will happen: "Pages stop until 16:16. The monitor keeps evaluating."), and a `vz-controls` with the yellow confirming button, whose label carries the choice ("Silence for 1 h"). Choosing a row only moves the tick; nothing happens until the button is pressed.
- **Where it opens.** Put the button that opens it and the menu in a `vz-menu-anchor`; the menu opens under the button with their left edges aligned (`data-open`), and above it (`data-flip`) when there is no room below. With a coarse pointer below 640px it is a sheet across the bottom of the screen, rows `control-touch` tall. It closes on Escape, on choosing a command, and on a press outside; focus returns to its button.
- **Row actions.** A table row's commands (Rename, Revoke key) are a menu opened by a quiet small button "Actions…" in the row's last cell (`td.vz-row-actions`), whose accessible name names the row ("Actions for ingest-eu"). Until the system ships icons, it is that word.
- Group with separators, not headings. The command palette is the same component with a labelled field above it.
- With a coarse pointer rows are `control-touch` tall.

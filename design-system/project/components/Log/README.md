What a program wrote, line by line. Question it answers: "what did the failing requests say, and which request was it?" A `vz-table vz-log` inside `vz-table-wrap`, under a `vz-log-tail` strip, in a panel under the search that produced it (`vz-query`).

- **Newest first** while the view is about now; oldest first when it is a record (an export, a finished job). The meta says which.
- **Columns**: the time (the key, `data`, with milliseconds: many lines share a second), the level, the message, then the labels that differ between lines (pod), then the trace as a link. A label every line shares (`service="checkout-api"`, or the level when the search names one) is not a column: the caption states it once, as the chart's legend note does.
- **Level**: `error` and `warn` carry their status mark and the word in `ink` (`vz-status`), because those lines need attention; `info` and `debug` are the word alone in `ink-secondary`. The level is never colour on the message.
- **The message wraps**, in `data`, keeping its own line breaks; nothing scrolls sideways, because the end of a line ("waited 250 ms") is as often the point as its start. After `clamp-lines` lines (4) it is cut, and a quiet small button under it says how much is hidden ("Show all 31 lines") and opens it in place (`aria-expanded`); only the rows below move. A message is never cut in the middle or at a fixed character count.
- **A matched search term** is `vz-match`: `ink`, the strong mono weight, on `rule`, like an insertion in a diff. Only what the search matched; never a colour.
- **Live tail.** The strip says how many lines are shown of how many, and whether the view is following. While the person is at the top with nothing open, focused or selected, new lines are added at most once a second, in one step, with no animation. Scrolling away, opening a line, selecting text or focusing inside the list pauses it: the meta says "paused at 15:16:02", the strip says "Paused while you read", and a button counts the lines held ("Show 214 newer lines", a real count). Pressing it adds them and keeps the line being read where it was on screen. The list never moves by itself.
- **Narrow**: `data-fold="wide"` (its columns do not fit 640 px: v5's `data-fold` scrolled 6 px at 200% zoom, review m15). Time and level stay on the first line; the message takes the full width on its own line at full size (it is what the list is for); the labels and the trace link fold under it.
- **States**: loading as Panel (the waiting line lies over the strip); empty says what was searched and what nearby search would find something ("It wrote 1,204 at error"), with that search as a button; error says which part failed and what still holds ("The chart above is complete; only the lines are missing"); a partial result says how many stores answered.
- **Touch**: the trace link and the "Show all" button are standalone controls with whole hit areas; a row's height grows with its message, never below `row`.

Presence test: the level column is dropped when the search names one level; the pod column is dropped when every line came from one pod.

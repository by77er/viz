- A 1px `ink` rule above the title is the only chrome. No box, fill or shadow.
- The head carries the title and, right-aligned, the unit and range in `ink-muted`.
- The description says what the reader should conclude from a change, in one or two sentences.
- Lines are 2px in `series-1` onward; gridlines `rule`; the baseline `rule-control`; axis text `ink-muted` in mono. Label up to four series directly and always include the legend.

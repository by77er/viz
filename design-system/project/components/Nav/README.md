The product's sections, across the top. `vz-nav`: the name, then links; the current one has `aria-current="page"` and is `ink` in `ui-strong`. The group at the right (the tenant, Settings) starts with a link carrying `vz-nav-end`, which pushes it to the right edge; no spacer element.

A link's focus ring is drawn inside it, because the strip that scrolls clips anything outside; the link keeps `space-1` round its words (its side padding given back by a negative margin, so nothing moves) and the ring stays a hairline clear of their letters, above and below too (v8: the sides; v9: the descender of "Explore" touched it at the fine pointer). `tools/audit.mjs` checks the ring against the ink of the words.

It scrolls sideways on narrow screens instead of collapsing into a menu, so every section stays one tap away; while there is more to its right, its right edge is a `rule-control` line, so an item cut at the edge reads as "more this way" (v5 review, m8). A tenant that is not a link (no tenant switch) is a `span.vz-nav-end.vz-secondary` (flows G23). Below 640px its gutter and gap are `space-4`, like the page's. The first focusable element of a page is a skip link (`a.vz-button.vz-skip`, "Skip to content"): it takes no space until it is focused, then it appears over the top left of the nav.

In running text, links are `vz-link`: `ink` with an underline that thickens on hover. Links are never blue.

Rows of comparable things. `vz-table` inside `vz-table-wrap`, with `vz-label` column heads on `sunken`.

- The identifying column is first, in `ui-strong` (`data-key`). If the row leads somewhere, the key holds the link, and **the link fills its cell**: the whole key cell is the target (in a folded row, the whole row; below). A long identifier is cut in the middle with `vz-cut` (its start and its distinguishing end, the whole in `title`), capped at half the measure, so the figures beside it stay on screen.
- Numbers are `vz-num`. A column of one unit is `vz-right` with the unit in its head ("p99, ms") and bare numbers in the cells, at every width the table is a table (`tools/audit.mjs` fails a column of bare numbers whose figures or head do not end at the cell's right edge, as rendered; v7 review, M2). A column of mixed units ("912 ms", "4 min 10 s", "1.3%") is left-aligned, each cell with its unit (see Number). A left-aligned column after a right-aligned one starts `space-6` in, so figures do not run into words.
- Rows are `row` tall and separated by `rule`; the hovered row is `hover`. No zebra striping, no vertical rules. **With a coarse pointer, rows that hold a link or a control are `control-touch` tall**, so a target in a row has its 44px without reaching into the next row; a table of figures with nothing to tap keeps its density.
- **The row whose detail is shown** (the trace open beside the list) is `aria-current` (or `aria-selected` in a selectable list): `rule` fill and an `ink` edge on its key, like a chosen segment. In the folded form the row, not its cells, carries the hover, chosen and pressed fill, so the fill covers the whole folded row and not only its cells.
- State is a `vz-status`, never a coloured cell. **Row actions** go in a menu: a quiet small button "Actions…" in the last cell (`td.vz-row-actions`, Menu).
- **Caption.** A table in a panel starts with `vz-table-caption`, one `small` line in `ink-secondary` that says how many rows of how many and in what order ("3 of 41 routes, slowest first"): the first number is the number of rows under it, and the rows are in the order it states (`tools/audit.mjs` checks both, and that an age beyond a day is a date and time). It is one run of text, so a `vz-qty` in it keeps its spaces. It is the view's strip: `space-6` tall, `control-touch` with a coarse pointer; a waiting line lies over it (Panel). A table that shows some of its rows always has one.
- **Sortable head**: a `button.vz-sort` inside the `th`; the `th` carries `aria-sort`. The sorted head is `ink` and strong with a triangle (up for ascending, down for descending); the triangle's space is always reserved, so sorting moves nothing. In a right-aligned column the triangle is on the left. Hover is `hover`, a press is `pressed`, painted at once. Say the initial order in the caption if it is not the first column's.
- **Wide table in a narrow region, two forms.** Choose per table:
  - *Scroll* (default): the wrap scrolls sideways and the first column stays in place. For tables people compare across many numeric columns.
  - *Fold* (`data-fold` on the table): below `breakpoint-narrow` of table width (`data-fold="wide"`: below `breakpoint-wide`, for a list whose columns do not fit in 640px, such as Monitors at 200% zoom), columns marked `data-priority="1"` (one or two, the ones the list exists for) stay as columns, and every other column folds into a line under them, in source order, each cell preceded by its `data-label` in `ink-muted` and its words in `ink`. Per row, v7 (v6 review, B1: with the actions on the key's line and the kept columns at their longest phrase, a pod name was left 87 px of a 368 px row and drawn over the CPU figures, and a monitor name wrapped to five lines):
    - **The key's line.** The key, then the kept columns, each `keep` wide (a quarter of the row) and never narrower than its longest phrase that cannot break; their width does not depend on their words, so they line up down the list. The key takes the rest and **never less than `key-min`** (half the row). Its link, and a `vz-cut` in it, shrink with it: a long identifier is cut in the middle (its distinguishing end kept) and a long name wraps inside the key. Nothing in the key is drawn outside it.
    - **Two kept columns below `breakpoint-narrow`.** A row cannot hold a readable key beside two columns there: the key takes the whole first line, and the kept columns open the second, under their heads (the head row folds the same way: the key's head, then the kept heads). With one kept column the key keeps its line beside it at every width.
    - **"Actions…"** (`td.vz-row-actions`) never shares the key's line, so it never takes the key's room: it ends the next line at the row's right edge (after the folded cells; after the kept columns when the key has its line to itself). With a coarse pointer, on the row's last line, that line is `control-touch` tall, so its whole hit area lies inside the row; in the middle line of a stacked row its hit area reaches into the row's own lines and the line keeps the figures' height. Nothing is hidden and nothing needs a second tap.
    - **The row is the key's target** (v8): a folded row is read and tapped as one item, so the key link's area is the whole row (other controls in it, "Actions…", lie above it), the row is at least `control-touch` tall and the key's first line is `space-2` from its top, so a finger on the name has 44 px of its own row around it. The key's line needs no touch height of its own: a stacked row of pods is about 92 px at 400 px with a coarse pointer (v7: about 120; v7 review, m1).
    - Write each quantity in a kept column as a `vz-qty`, and a pair of phrases as `vz-phrase` spans ("&gt; 500 ms" "for 5 min"), so a narrow column wraps between phrases, the same way in every row. A state in a kept column wraps between words, its mark on the first line. Only the key's head stays sortable in the folded form. There is no gutter after a right-aligned column once the columns are folded.
    - **A folding table never scrolls sideways**: if its columns do not fit 640 px (at 200% zoom a window of 1440 px is 720), it is `data-fold="wide"`. For lists a person reads row by row, especially on a phone: monitors, dashboards, keys, pods. Mark as priority 1 the one or two columns the list exists for ("Now", "Threshold"; "CPU", "Throttled"), not the ones that are merely short.
    - **Checked as rendered** (`tools/audit.mjs`, at every width from 320 px, at 200% zoom and with a coarse pointer): no word's centre belongs to another element (a name drawn over the figures, or a cell drawn over the name), no key is narrower than `key-min` of its row, a kept state wraps, nothing is cut by its cell, the table never scrolls sideways. The composition `reviews/system-v7/compositions/folded-list.html` (40 pods, four monitors with real names, row actions) is the case.
  - Give a folding table explicit roles (`role="table"`, `row`, `columnheader`, `cell`), because the folded layout replaces the table display.
  - A row expander was considered and not used: it hides the columns behind a tap per row, which is the wrong cost for a person scanning a list at night.
- The page never scrolls sideways. The wrap is positioned, so anything absolutely placed inside the table (a visually hidden head, a key link's hit area) is clipped by its scroll and never widens the page (v5 review, B2.1). While it can scroll further, its right edge is a `rule-control` line (its own border, so no cell covers it).

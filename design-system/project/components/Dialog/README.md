A decision that must be made before anything else. `vz-dialog`: a title that states the decision as a question or a verb phrase, the consequences in one or two sentences, any input the decision needs, and the actions at the bottom right.

- Use it only when the person cannot sensibly continue without answering, or when an action cannot be undone. Everything else is edited in place.
- The confirming button repeats the verb from the title ("Delete dashboard"), in the tone of the action. Cancel is quiet and comes first.
- Focus moves into the dialog on open and returns to the control that opened it on close. Escape cancels.
- It is `dialog` wide and never wider than the screen; on narrow screens the actions wrap.

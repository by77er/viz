One trace's spans: where one request's time went, what ran side by side and what waited on what. Question it answers: "this request was slow; which part of it took the time?" A `vz-table vz-waterfall` with `role="treegrid"` inside `vz-table-wrap`, under a `vz-table-caption`, in a panel whose title names the trace and whose meta gives its start, length and span count.

- **Rows are spans in the order they started**, each indented by its depth (`aria-level` on the row; `space-4` a level, `space-3` in the narrow form). Depth is shown to the ninth level; deeper spans keep that indent and the accessible tree carries the true level.
- **Columns**: the span (`service: operation`, the key), "Took, ms", "Own, ms", the bar, "Starts at, ms". **Own time** is the span's length less the time covered by the spans inside it: the number that says whether a span was slow itself or only waited on others. Figures are bare, the unit in the head.
- **The bar** is the span's start and length as fractions of the trace, set on the bar as `--at` and `--len` (0 to 1). They are data, written by whatever draws the rows, as a chart writes its coordinates; they are the only inline style the component takes. The column head is the scale: the trace's start "0" and its length "1,204 ms", in `micro`. A span shorter than a `stroke` is drawn a `stroke` wide, so it is never invisible. The column is half the table at rest and the table's full width, on a line of its own, in the narrow form, so every bar keeps one scale.
- **Where the time went**: a span whose own time is a tenth of the trace or more is drawn in `ink` with its name strong; every other bar is `ink-muted`. Lightness and weight, not colour: a span is not a series and its length is not a state, so series, action and status colours are never used for bars. The panel's sentence says the same in words ("payments-db took 957 ms of the 1,204 ms, in two calls").
- **A failed span** carries the critical mark before its name, and its accessible name says "failed". A collapsed span says how many spans it holds and how many of those failed, with the mark ("3 spans inside, 1 failed"), so a failure is never hidden by collapsing.
- **Collapse**: a span with spans inside has a toggle before its name (`vz-tree-toggle`, a triangle pointing at what it would open, `aria-expanded`); leaves have a gap of the same width, so names line up. "N spans inside" is shown only while they are hidden. **A trace of more than 200 spans opens collapsed** except the spans that lead to each span drawn in `ink`, and the caption says how many are shown ("2,140 spans, 23 shown"); rows inside a collapsed span are not rendered.
- **A span's own target**: the name is a button over its whole cell. It makes the row the one whose detail is shown (`aria-selected`: `rule` fill, `ink` edge on the key; Table) and shows the span's facts under the table as a `vz-kv`, with its standalone links ("Logs from this span"). The toggle sits above it and is its own target.
- **Keyboard**: the tree is one tab stop. Up and Down move between visible spans, Home and End to the ends, Right opens a span or goes into it, Left closes it or goes to its parent, Enter or Space shows its detail. The focus ring is round the whole row.
- **Touch**: rows are `control-touch` tall (they hold controls), the toggle is `control-touch` square, so the toggle and the name never share a hit area.
- **Narrow**: `data-fold="wide"` (its five columns do not fit 640 px; with `data-fold` it scrolled at 200% zoom and in a split column at 1500 px). The span and "Took" stay as columns, the bar takes its own line under them, and "Own" and "Starts at" fold under it with their labels.
- **States**: loading and stale as Panel; error: `vz-empty` with the critical mark in the table's place, saying which part is missing ("The list of slow traces is complete; only this trace's spans are missing"); a trace still being written says how many spans have arrived in the caption.
- **Text form**: the panel's sentence (where the time went), then one line per span drawn in `ink` with its start, length and own time, then the failures.

Presence test: the scale head answers "how long is the whole bar"; Own answers "slow itself, or waiting"; the bar answers "when, and beside what". A Gantt-style grid of time lines was considered and not drawn: the figures are in the columns, and lines across every row would compete with the bars.

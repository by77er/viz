Does a reload come back to the same place?

A test of the viz design system v9 pages on a real phone, about two minutes. Every page holds mock data only.

For each page below, three times

  1. Open the page. Scroll to the very bottom with your finger.
  2. Note what is at the top of the screen: the chart, the heading or the line of text.
  3. Press the browser's reload button. Wait two seconds without touching the screen.
  4. Note whether the page comes back at the same place, or somewhere else. If it is somewhere else, note roughly how far: half a screen, one screen, several screens.

Then send back, per page: the phone, the browser, and for each of the three tries "same place" or "somewhere else" (and how far).

Pages

  1. Chart preview (the page the review measured most: 22 of 34 reloads landed short in an emulated phone)
  2. Reference (every component, very long)
  3. Charts composition
  4. Dashboard composition

If you have time: on the Chart preview, repeat the test about 20 times. If it ever comes back somewhere else, that is enough to know it is real.
